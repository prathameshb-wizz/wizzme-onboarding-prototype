1 · What this is, and what ships

This onboarding replaces the current sign-up with a share-first arc: a short animated film makes the case that the user's lived experience is valuable and that sharing it is effortless on Wizzme; sign-up happens at the emotional peak; and the very first in-app action is answering three light interview questions that become the user's first story ("painting"), hung on their profile before they ever see a feed.

The build divides cleanly in two:

  • Part A: the film (scenes 1–7). Pure presentation. No user data, no backend. Ship it as a GSAP timeline (the prototype's implementation can be ported nearly as-is into a React component), or pre-render it to a video/Lottie if bundle size wins. Tap/click advances; it also auto-advances.
  • Part B: the app (screens 8–13). Real product: auth, profile basics, topic selection, the answering engine, story generation, and the story reveal. These need routes, state, and backend endpoints.
Locked decisions (do not re-litigate in implementation)
  • The answering engine reuses the existing Sharer interview engine: same chip-driven Q&A interaction, restyled per screen 11 below. Do not build a second engine.
  • Message architecture of the film (the seven-beat narrative and its copy) is final.
  • No em dashes anywhere in copy. No competitor platform names. All example people are two-part real names.
  • Story visibility defaults to Connections, changeable at the reveal (Private / Connections / Public).

5 · Part B · The app screens (8–13), full specs

Screen 8 · The basics

RouteAuth required Entered after Google sign-up (Supabase Auth, existing integration in src/integrations/supabase/).

  • Heading "First, the basics." + subline "This stays on your profile, nothing else does."
  • Fields: Your name (text, prefilled from Google profile), Date of birth (date input, prototype shows DD / MM / YYYY), Pronouns as single-select chips: She / her · He / him · They / them, plus a quiet underlined text link "Prefer not to say" below the chips (it is deliberately NOT styled as a fourth chip; clicking it continues without a pronoun).
  • Primary CTA Continue → screen 9. Validation: name non-empty; DOB optional-valid; pronouns optional.

Screen 9 · Topics

"What could you talk about without preparing?" + "Pick a few. One story now, the rest stay saved."

  • Grid (2-col) of 8 topic cards, each title + sub: College days (the friends, the chaos) · Office life (first jobs, bosses, growth) · Moving cities (flats, food, finding your feet) · Relationships (friends, dating, family) · Money (salaries, saving, mistakes) · Travel (solo trips, plans gone wrong) · Getting fit (around everything else) · Interests (startups, sports, hobbies).
  • Free-text input "Something else? Type it here": non-empty counts as a selection.
  • Selection cap: 4. CTA is disabled AND visually muted (grey fill, not just opacity) until ≥1 selection; label switches "Pick a few to continue" → "Continue with N".
  • Routing rule: exactly 1 selection → set it as the chosen topic and go directly to screen 11 (skip 10). 2+ → screen 10.
  • State restore: navigating back to this screen re-renders prior selections as selected and the CTA live.

Screen 10 · Which one first

  • "Which one first?" + "Start with whichever you are in the mood for." Rows for each selected topic (label + sub + chevron); tapping one sets the chosen topic → screen 11. Footnote: "The rest stay saved for later."

Screen 11 · Answering (the interview engine)

Reuses Sharer engine Three steps per story: one topic question + two follow-ups. Restyle per below; interaction logic is the existing engine's.

  • Hero (148px): topic cover art, blurred 7px + saturate 1.25, gradient scrim (.62→.28→.12). Overlaid header: 44px ringed avatar, topic name (serif 18px bold) + "N answered so far ⌄", right-aligned pill with brush icon wag animation + "painting your cover". Bottom: frosted paint-progress bar, width 15% + 30% per answered step.
  • Sheet (#FAF7F3, radius 28): grab handle, orb + Kalam coaching line rotating per step: "Say it just how it comes out." / "No need to polish it." / "Last one, then I shape it.", question (serif, 23px), help line "Pick the one that fits best.", answer chips (44px min height; tap → selected grey state → 650ms pause → next question, or → screen 12 after step 3).
  • Bar: mic and attachment circle buttons (voice/photo answers, wire to existing capabilities or stub), right-aligned "Skip" (skips the current question, not the flow).
  • First questions per topic (final copy): college: "What is the one college memory you still tell people about?" · work: "What caught you off guard in your first job?" · moving: "What did nobody warn you about moving to a new city?" · rel: "What has dating in your twenties actually taught you?" · money: "What did your first salary actually teach you?" · travel: "What did your first solo trip change in you?" · fitness: "What finally made fitness stick for you?" · interests: "What could you talk about for hours without notes?" · free text: "What is the story you tell most often about this?" Follow-ups: "What made it stick with you?" then "And has that changed how you act now?"
  • Phone chrome (brand + film dashes) is hidden on this screen and all later ones: the hero owns the top.

Screen 12 · Buffer (story is being made)

~9.5s, three acts under a status line with a pulsing dot; this is where the backend generates the story and cover.

  1. "Reading what you painted": the user's three answers laid down as small rotated cards.
  2. "Painting your cover": answers sweep away; an empty canvas takes colour washes that resolve into a blurred version of the actual final cover (blur 16→5). The thing being painted must be the thing revealed on screen 13.
  3. "Finding who needs it": you → arrow → three avatars row.

Treat the animation as a minimum-duration mask over the real generation call: if generation finishes early, hold to act timing; if late, loop act 2 washes until ready.

Screen 13 · Your story (reveal)

  • Full-bleed cover art background (blur 40 + dark veil), label "YOU PAINTED SOMETHING", heading "Your answers / became a story".
  • Story card: cover (with the white circular quote badge, top-left), byline "<Topic>, in your words" (Kalam), title = the user's first answer, body composed from the question + answers (LLM-composed in production; the prototype shows the template shape).
  • Visibility pill: "Visible to Connections ⌄" opens a real menu: Private / Connections / Public, default Connections, selection updates the pill.
  • CTA "Hang it on my profile" → persists story + visibility, lands in the app proper.
  • Cover art follows the chosen topic (topic→art mapping; free-text topics get a default until a cover pipeline exists).

8 · Motion spec

  • Engine: GSAP 3 timelines; one timeline per scene, rebuilt on entry. Word-stagger text reveals (~.07–.09s/word) with highlight underlines painting in after the word lands; typing effects at ~35–40ms/char, grouped into non-breaking word spans so lines never wrap mid-word.
  • Signature easings: entrances power2/power3.out or back.out(1.3–1.6); exits power2.in; drifts linear. Deck cards: peek at corner (opacity .28) → enter (.85s) → linear drift through centre (2.4s) → accelerate out (.75s).
  • One-moving-thing rule: at any moment exactly one element should demand the eye. This is deliberate throughout scenes 1 and 7 and must be preserved in any re-implementation.
  • Auto-advance: film scenes advance on tap and automatically (600ms after scene end; scene 5 holds 2200ms; scene 6→7 hands over in 400ms). All film timelines run at 1.1× timeScale; total time to the sign-up CTA is ~45s. Arrow keys navigate everywhere.
  • Reduced motion: the product build must respect prefers-reduced-motion via useReducedMotion() from @/lib/animations: film falls back to static frames with tap-through; app screens drop non-essential animation.

10 · Analytics

Screens are auto-tracked once routes exist (add entries to src/lib/analytics/screens.ts). Custom events via track() with a typed catalog (suggest onboardingEvents.ts), naming <domain>_<object>_<action>:

  • onboarding_film_scene_viewed (scene_index), onboarding_film_skipped, onboarding_deck_card_viewed (story_id), onboarding_signup_clicked
  • onboarding_basics_submitted (pronouns_set: bool), onboarding_topics_submitted (count, has_free_text), onboarding_topic_started (topic)
  • onboarding_answer_submitted (step, input_type: chip|text|voice), onboarding_question_skipped (step)
  • onboarding_story_generated (latency_ms), onboarding_story_visibility_set (visibility), onboarding_story_hung

No PII or free-text answer content in event props; ids only. Session replay stays global per repo policy.

12 · Acceptance criteria and QA checklist

  • Film: every scene follows the one-moving-thing rule; scene 3's typing completes before its closing line; scene 5 comment is fully readable before advance; scene 6→7 brand hands over (no abrupt blink); scene 7 order is PRESENTING → logo + subtitle → caption → cards (subtitles keep rotating alongside) → canvas → CTA.
  • Topics: CTA visually disabled at zero picks; single pick lands directly in answering with the right topic in the hero; returning restores picks; cap at 4 enforced.
  • Answering: 3 steps then buffer; skip skips a question only; progress bar reflects steps; chrome hidden.
  • Buffer: what is painted is the cover that is revealed; generation latency masked (never a spinner).
  • Reveal: visibility menu works and persists; title echoes the user's first answer; "Hang it on my profile" persists and exits onboarding.
  • All data screens handle loading / empty / error states per repo standards (Skeleton, EmptyState, ErrorState).
  • Responsive at 375px and 1280px; touch targets ≥44px; visible focus states; prefers-reduced-motion respected; no console errors; every screen registered in screens.ts; events fire per §10.
  • Copy: no em dashes, no competitor names, verbatim quotes on deck cards, singular/plural correct ("1 story").

13 · Open items and flags

  • Deck consent: confirm the four authors are fine appearing on a pre-signup marketing surface (all four stories are public; this is policy, not tech).
  • Cover pipeline: v1 topic→art mapping ships with 4+3 fixed paintings; per-story generated covers are v2. Free-text topics need a default cover.
  • Voice/photo answers: mic + attach buttons exist in the answering bar; wire to existing capture or ship disabled in v1.
  • Film delivery decision: GSAP-in-React vs pre-rendered video. GSAP keeps tap-to-advance and crisp text; video is cheaper to maintain. Prototype timings transfer either way.
  • Shivani's video essay ("jugnu"): the strongest content in the pool needs a video-card treatment; parked as a possible fifth deck idea.
  • RCB jersey story: excluded pending founder approval (names the co-founder).
